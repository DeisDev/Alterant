<h1 align="center">Alterant</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Keep your world just right.</strong></p>

<p align="center">Minecraft 26.2 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Fabric &amp; NeoForge &nbsp;·&nbsp; Client &amp; Server</p>

---

Keep vines from spreading, coral alive on dry land, and sand suspended in midair. **Alterant** adds craftable coatings, tools and time serums to control how individual blocks grow, change and work.

## A coating for every project

| Coating | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Growth Inhibitor** | Stops natural growth and spreading on supported plants. |
| **Growth Regulator** | Sets a growth stage for supported crops or a height limit for bamboo and sugar cane. |
| **Preserving Sealant** | Prevents melting, leaf decay, copper weathering and coral death. |
| **Structural Stasis** | Holds falling blocks in place and preserves stair, fence and wall shapes. |
| **Temporal Stasis** | Pauses standard block and machine activity while keeping their contents intact. |
| **Transfer Seal** | Seals supported item and fluid inputs, outputs or selected faces while machines keep processing. |

Coatings stay until removed. Your original blocks and inventories stay in place.

## A little extra time

Give supported crops and machines a temporary speed boost with **Time Serum** and the **Quantum Applicator**. Choose **Refined**, **Enduring** or **Overcharged** serums for more speed or a longer boost, or try **Suspicious Time Serum** for a random strength.

Serum timers pause when their chunks stop ticking.

## The finishing touches

- **Paint a surface.** Switch between one block and a 3×3 area. Use **Masking Strips** to leave parts untouched.
- **Shape the details.** Preview, preserve and copy stair, fence and wall shapes with the **Shaping Stylus**.
- **Rework and reclaim.** Remove coatings with a **Scraper**, collect residue in **Reclamation Jars**, and recover ingredients in the **Reclaiming Basin** or crafting grid. **Release Solvent** dissolves coatings without recovering residue.

## Getting started

Hold a **Preserving Brush** in your main hand and a compound jar in your offhand, then use the brush on a block. **Sneak-use in the air** to switch between single-block and 3×3 modes. For serums, use the **Quantum Applicator** with a serum dose in your offhand.

<details>
<summary><strong>Installation &amp; compatibility</strong></summary>

- **Minecraft 26.2 · Java 25.** Install the matching Fabric or NeoForge version on both the client and server.
- **Fabric requires [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).**
- **Optional integrations:** Jade for treatment details and live serum timing; YACL for configuration screens, with Mod Menu on Fabric. Live Jade timing needs Jade on both sides.
- **Modded machines:** coverage depends on how each machine works. External networks and independent timers may continue running during stasis; serums accelerate supported block activity only.
