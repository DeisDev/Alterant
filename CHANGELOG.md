# Changelog

Preserve is unreleased and under active development.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Recover residue from paid coatings with careful scraping.
- Collect residue in Reclamation Jars and reclaim ingredients in the Reclaiming Basin or crafting grid.
- Mark decorative patterns with Masking Strips to skip area treatments.
- Dissolve coatings with Release Solvent by hand, across a surface or from a dispenser.
- Set growth limits for supported crops, bamboo and sugar cane.
- Preview, preserve and copy decorative shapes with the Shaping Stylus.
- Seal machine inputs, outputs or chosen faces while keeping processing and inventory access.
- Refined, Enduring and Overcharged Time Serums.
- Chronal Alloy, Echo Matrix and Dragonbound Catalyst crafting materials.
- Settings for each serum's speed, duration and Suspicious Serum chances.

### Changed

- Basic display keeps world feedback quiet; detailed panels are available in Advanced.
- Rebalanced serum durations and Suspicious Serum rolls.
- Removed the old serum settings format.
